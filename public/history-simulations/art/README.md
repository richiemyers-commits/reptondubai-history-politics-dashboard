# History Command artwork

Original AI-generated illustrative reconstructions, created for Repton Dubai’s KS3 games with the built-in image generation workflow. These are not archival photographs, authenticated portraits or exact battlefield reconstructions. No Paradox game assets, logos or interface artwork are reproduced.

All six WebP files are 1280 × 853 pixels, optimised at quality 83 from 1536 × 1024 source images. They support campaign cards, briefing heroes and compact in-game scenes. Schematic maps remain the navigational graphics.

## Shared prompt direction

Historical-scene key art; painterly realism, believable anatomy and period materials; cinematic natural light; muted navy, olive, ochre and antique gold; subtle oil-paint brushwork; atmospheric depth. Landscape 3:2 composition, principal subjects in the centre/right and calmer background at left for readable text. No words, logos, UI, watermark or modern objects. Serious human perspectives and historically plausible clothing and equipment.

## Scene prompts

- `normans.webp`: two Anglo-Saxon earls in mail and wool cloaks considering a parchment beside river meadows near York in September 1066; English followers with round shields, spears and nasal helmets; no plate armour or horned helmets.
- `milltown.webp`: working people, including a woman and older child, beside a riverside textile mill town around 1835–1845; red-brick mills, chimneys, canal and modest housing; early morning light and smoke; industrial opportunity and difficult conditions together.
- `ganga.webp`: representative 57th Wilde’s Rifles soldiers and a supply carrier in northern France in late 1914; khaki wool, puttees, leather equipment and military turbans; muddy shallow trenches and autumn fields; no steel helmets, gas masks, camels or Maharaja clothing.
- `algeria.webp`: Algerian tirailleurs helping a weary comrade toward a rally point near Ypres in April 1915; plausible early-war clothing and chechia headwear; muddy trenches and distant ominous haze; no modern masks, graphic injury or celebratory violence.
- `mike.webp`: a representative Kainai man in standard Canadian Expeditionary Force uniform with comrades on the Western Front in 1917–1918; khaki wool, Brodie helmet and period equipment; no stereotyped headdress or invented ceremonial objects. Not an authenticated likeness of Mike Mountain Horse.
- `clc.webp`: Chinese Labour Corps workers maintaining railway infrastructure and moving supplies near a repair shed in France or Belgium around 1917–1918; blue-grey work clothing and cloth caps; period tools and transport; dignified skilled work, no rifles or infantry assault.

## Use and interpretation

Art is labelled “Illustrative reconstruction” in the games. Images support atmosphere, not factual inference about specific individuals. Historical statements, source evaluation and explicit reconstruction boundaries remain in the game text.
